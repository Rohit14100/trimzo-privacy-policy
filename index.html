Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TriMzo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how TriMzo collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our salon booking application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and username
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Account authentication information
  • User role: Customer, Salon Owner or Staff
  • Address and profile photo URL

Location Information

  • Device location when location permission is granted.
  • City and state information derived from device coordinates.
  • Salon latitude and longitude provided by salon owners.

Booking Information

  • Selected salon, services and staff member
  • Booking date and appointment time
  • Appointment duration and booking status
  • Booking completion verification information

Payment Information

  • Payment method and payment amount
  • Payment status
  • Offline payment method, such as cash or UPI
  • Payment and refund reference details, where applicable

Razorpay online payment processing is not currently activated.

Reviews and Ratings

  • Customer ratings and review comments
  • Related salon and booking information

Device and Notification Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging device token
  • Notification permission status
  • Notification title, message and read status

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify users through OTP authentication.
  • Manage salon profiles and services.
  • Enable and manage salon appointment bookings.
  • Manage staff and appointment schedules.
  • Send booking-related notifications.
  • Display salon location information.
  • Manage customer reviews and ratings.
  • Maintain application security and functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

MSG91: Used for OTP-based phone number verification.

Firebase Cloud Messaging: Used to deliver push notifications. Device tokens may be stored on our backend and associated with user accounts.

Google Maps and Location Services: Used for map-related functionality and location features.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide booking services, deliver authentication and notification services, operate integrated service providers, comply with legal requirements or protect the rights and security of users and TriMzo.

6. Data Storage and Security

User and application information is stored and processed through our backend infrastructure and integrated service providers. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain booking records, meet operational requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Users may request account and associated personal data deletion by contacting:

Email: trimzoa@gmail.com

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed subject to applicable legal and legitimate record-retention requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

TriMzo is a salon booking platform. The availability of salon categories such as Kids does not mean that the application is specifically intended for children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of salon booking services.

9. User Permissions

Depending on the features used, TriMzo may request location and notification permissions. Users can manage these permissions through their device settings. Disabling permissions may affect related application features.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published with a revised Last Updated date.
